TURF ARENA - RULES & POLICIES

1. CANCELATION POLICY:
   - Cancellations made 24 hours prior to the slot receive a 100% full refund.
   - Cancellations within 24 hours receive a 50% refund.
   - No-shows receive zero refund.

2. FOOTWEAR & EQUIPMENT:
   - Only molded rubber studs or flat-soled turf shoes are allowed on the pitch.
   - Metal studs are strictly forbidden to protect the turf surface.

3. AMENITIES & PARKING:
   - Free parking is available for up to 30 cars and 50 two-wheelers.
   - Changing rooms, drinking water, and floodlights are included in the hourly rate.
